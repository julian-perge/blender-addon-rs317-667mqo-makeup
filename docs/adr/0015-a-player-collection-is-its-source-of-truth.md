# A player's collection and stored settings are its source of truth

A player is a collection holding its meshes, its rig and its stored appearance settings. These are the owner's choices, made from 2026-11-07 to 2026-11-09:

- The collection's stored settings are the source of truth. The panel records which player it belongs to: Create, a successful Update and Load Player Settings tie it to a player, and loading a preset file unties it. Update refuses, before any change, when the panel is tied to another existing player.
- Selecting any object of another player (body, weapon, shield, held item or rig) loads that player's stored settings into the panel and ties the panel to it (2026-11-09). Settings the panel holds that no player stores as they are stay in the panel when a player is selected: changes not yet applied with Update, a preset file's settings and a new scene's settings. A note then names both ways on, Update or Load Player Settings. A panel tied to a player since deleted or renamed counts as tied to no player, following the rule for preset files.
- An edit fingerprint tells an edited player from an unedited one. Update never overwrites a hand-edited mesh or rig ([plan 06](../../tasks/old/osrs-focus/plans/06-charactercreator-m4.md), decision 4, which allowed refusing or copying): it rebuilds an unedited player in place, and keeps an edited one and builds the update as a copy beside it. Update carries what the add-on did not make (issue 27, decided 2026-11-10; the rules below for parents, constraints, parent collections and view layer settings were added 2026-11-11). A rebuilt player takes over the child collections and the objects that are neither meshes nor armatures that the old player held. An edited player keeps them, and its copy starts without them. Objects parented or constrained to the old player's meshes or rig follow to the rebuilt ones, or lose their parent where the rebuild has no counterpart, and keep their place in the world. The rebuilt player keeps the old player's parent collections and view layer settings, and the carried collections and objects keep theirs. Removing a player still refuses while it holds them. Moving, turning or scaling a whole player is not an edit, and moving one of its meshes against the rig is (2026-11-09).
- When the fingerprint's formula changes, players saved before count as edited: the bakes refuse them, and Update builds a fresh copy beside them.
