# A Claude-made motion is tested before any toolkit code is written

On 2026-11-12 the owner settled the design of step 1 of issue 12 (hand-made motion for players) in the Design interview. The issue's idea comment splits the work into two steps. Step 1 has Claude key a motion on a player's rig through the Blender MCP. Step 2 turns the motion into a text file that a third clip source feeds to the bakes. These decisions cover step 1 only. Step 2 gets its own Design stage.

- The test comes before toolkit code. Claude keys a sword flourish of 1 to 2 seconds on the joints of an existing player, on the "Your motion" track, and checks its work on contact sheets from a scratch script. The alternative was to build the contact sheet operator and the runbook first and test with them. The owner rejected it, because a failed test would then leave toolkit code without a use.
- The flourish plays over the baked stand clip of the worn sword, and starts and ends at the stand pose. A motion from the rest pose would not test the "Your motion" track over a baked clip. A motion over a moving clip such as walk needs mixing per joint, which the idea comment left out.
- The flourish passes when, beside a baked attack clip of the same player, it could be a real Old School clip: stepped poses held for whole client ticks, rigid body parts, the sword fixed in the hand, and nothing detached or passing through the body. The owner judges the render.
- The owner reviews at most three renders. If the third fails, the test fails, the issue records which assumption broke (15 joints, still-image feedback or the sword in the hand), and step 2 does not start.
- After a pass, the contact sheet becomes a toolkit operator that ships in the extension, and the runbook goes in the repo's docs. The audience is any user of the extension who has Claude and the Blender MCP, not only the owner. The test shows what the operator and the runbook need, and the contact sheet layout is left to it.
